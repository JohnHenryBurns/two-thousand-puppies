2000 Puppies!

for

You have been alive for 2,000 days.
That is a LOT of days!

So here are 2,000 puppies. One for every single day. Every puppy has a name, and every puppy is yours.

For grown-ups

The puppies are numbered by day: puppy #1 is the day she was born, puppy #2000 is day 2,000. Set the birthday so each puppy shows its real date. For a friend who is a different number of days old, change the days (up to 2,600, about 7 years) and the page reloads with that many puppies. Everything is saved on this device. A link works too: ?name=Ada&days=1846 for a fixed number, or ?name=Ada&birthday=2021-09-05 to count the days from that birthday to today.

How to play

  • 🌍 Far away, the puppies are tiny dots. Wave your finger or mouse through them to shoo them!
  • 🔍 Zoom in with two fingers, the scroll wheel, or the ➕ button to get close.
  • ✋ Up close, tap a puppy to pet it. Drag on the grass to look around.
  • 📣 Call: press and hold, and the puppies come running.
  • 🎁 Play: pick a 🦴 Treat (tap to drop one), the 🎾 Ball (tap to throw, a puppy fetches it back), or a 📻 Dance party (tap to put down the boombox and the puppies nearby dance).
  • Commands: tap a puppy, then press Sit, Lie down, Roll over or Beg on its card.
  • ✨ Surprise: watch all 2,000 puppies make a shape.
  • Puppies sometimes need a potty break. Mind the flies. 🪰
  • The little map in the corner shows all 2,000 puppies. Tap it to jump there.

How big is 2,000?

  • If you pet one puppy every second and never stop, it takes 33 minutes to pet them all.
  • 2,000 puppies nose to tail would be as long as 8 football fields.
  • 2,000 days is more than 5 years. And you lived every one of them!
